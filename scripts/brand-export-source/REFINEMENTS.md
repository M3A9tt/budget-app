## A prompt

Use case: logo-brand, precise identity refinement. Reference 1 is the approved standalone circular green/lavender currency-Б symbol. Reference 2 is the approved handwritten suffix юрджет. Output one PRODUCTION LOGO asset, genuinely transparent background, tightly framed horizontal composition, no labels, no alternate views, no mockup, no portrait. Read exactly Бюрджет: the reference symbol IS the first Б, followed ONLY by юрджет. Retain the recognisable original folded Б, sage upper section, lavender lower section, two currency bars and delicate circle. Retain the third-direction handwritten shapes and rhythm of юрджет, excellent Cyrillic spelling. Make this look genuinely composed by an experienced identity designer, not an icon pasted onto text. Optically balance the ink heights: the capital Б body is only about 15–20 percent taller than the body of ю, not nearly double. The ring extends modestly above/below the word. Align the actual letter baselines and visual weight, keep compact width and natural kerning. Preserve open counters and letter recognition. The suffix letters рджет are FLAT SOLID off-white #EDF2F7: no chrome, no grey outlines, no silver bevel, no texture, no shadows. Depth is restrained to the emblem and fades out through the first entry stroke of ю. No underlines, no long connecting ribbon, no sweeping swoosh underneath the name, no ornamental tail, no glowing rim, no decorative bridge or extra line. This is a logo refinement, not new typography or a new brand. No replacement by Latin B, Bitcoin, dollar sign or a serif glyph. Variant A, refined short junction: reduce the overlarge badge dominance, move the first ю close to the right-hand curve of Б. Make a VERY SHORT quietly tapered connector directly from the right exit of the Б bowl into the left entry of ю at the letter-body junction. The connector must be a natural continuation of the actual letter stroke, only a small fraction of one letter width. It must NOT grow from the bottom ring or travel below the baseline. The circular outline remains a simple circle; where the letter junction crosses it on the right, let the stroke sit in front and hide just that tiny ring segment. Blend lavender to near-white over this short junction only. Prioritize near-invisible craft: a compact, confident, highly legible single name with a quiet connection, not a fancy effect. Keep the handwritten д flourish modest and make final т clear at small scale.
